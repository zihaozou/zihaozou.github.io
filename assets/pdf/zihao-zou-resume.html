Research Interests

My research focuses on developing robust and efficient frameworks for computational photography and scientific imaging with GenAI. In particular, I work on high-speed, high-quality 3D imaging through multi-modal sensor fusion and optics design. I also have broad interests in imaging inverse problems, generative AI, and metalens design.

Keywords: Computer Vision, Computational Imaging, Large-scale Optimization, Machine Learning, Event Camera, TOF Camera, Camera Fusion, Diffusion Models for Imaging, Metalens Design.
